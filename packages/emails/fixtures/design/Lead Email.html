Questions people ask us first

I have no documents at all. Is it worth continuing?

Yes. Most families start with nothing but a name and a country. We search the German and Austrian registries and state archives for the records, including files families believed were lost.

Does adding my information commit me to anything?

No. Completing the application and uploading records costs nothing and carries no obligation. You see the fee before any paid work begins, and you decide then.

We were told years ago that we did not qualify.

That may no longer hold. Both the German and Austrian provisions widened in recent years, and families refused under the older rules are often eligible now.

Who sees what I upload?

Only the lawyers and case managers working on your file. Your documents are held confidentially by the firm and removed on request.

How long does a case take?

It depends on the archives and the consulate, not on us. After reading your file, a lawyer gives you a realistic timeline for your route rather than an average.
