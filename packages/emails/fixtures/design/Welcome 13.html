A short glossary

David, five words worth knowing.

Citizenship by descent comes with its own vocabulary.
Most of it is simpler than it sounds.

In plain English

Citizenship by descent

A right that passes down a family line.
You are not applying for something new, you are asking a state to recognise something that was already yours.

Restoration

The route for families whose citizenship was taken away during the Nazi era.
Both countries we work in have provisions for exactly these descendants.

The descent chain

The documented line from your ancestor to you.
Every birth and marriage in between has to be evidenced, which is the part that takes the work.

Certified translation

A translation an authority will accept.
It has to come from a recognised translator, never from you or from an app.

Apostille

A stamp that makes a document from one country valid in another.
We arrange these for you.

You will not be asked to know any of this.

It is here so that nothing in your file reads like a foreign language when it reaches you.
