For the next generation

David, this is rarely only about you.

Most people who start this process are thinking a generation ahead.

Here is what the citizenship itself carries.

What a second citizenship opens

Live

The right to reside in any European Union member state, not only in Germany or Austria.

Work

Employment across the union without a visa or a sponsor.

Study

University on the same terms as local students, which in much of Europe means little or no tuition.

Travel

A European passport and the border treatment that comes with it.

Pass it on

Citizenship by descent continues to your children, which is the part most families are really deciding about.

None of it is automatic, and nothing here is a promise about your own case.

It is what the citizenship itself carries, once a case is accepted.
